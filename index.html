FindHide AI

Find any photo by describing it. Lock private ones away. Fully offline.

FindHide AI is a photo gallery for Android that understands what is in your pictures, and a vault that keeps the private ones locked away. Everything happens on your phone: the app does not ask for internet access, has no ads and no tracking.

  • Find: type what you remember, such as "dog on the beach" or "birthday cake", and the photos appear.
  • Hide: move photos and videos into an encrypted private album that only you can open.
  • Tidy: duplicates, blurred shots, automatic categories, a recycle bin.
